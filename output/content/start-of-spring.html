Original paragraph (cleaned up)

“In simple words — Lohri is considered end of winter. Till end of winter days are smaller and colder. Next day Sankranti marks the beginning of longer days and warmer and longer days compared to the nights. In other words, Sankranti marks the end of winter season and beginning of a new harvest or spring season.” fileciteturn6file0

Simple explanation you can share

A modern, easy-to-read version of the same idea.

The “light” starts winning

People feel the days slowly getting longer — so Sankranti becomes a symbol of hope and renewal.

Families gather

It’s a comfortable time to meet family, share food, speak kindly, and start the season with gratitude.

Kites, sweets, harvest

Rooftops fill with kites, plates fill with sesame-jaggery sweets, and the harvest becomes a reason to celebrate.

One-line caption
