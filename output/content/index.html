Sun Transition

“Makar Sankranti” comes from Makara (Capricorn) + Sankranti (transition). The day is also called Uttarayan as the sun begins its northward journey, marking longer days ahead.

Multiple Names

  • Pongal
  • Uttaryan
  • Magh Bihu
  • Khichdi Purv

A festival of feasting, family gatherings, and gratitude celebrated across India and beyond.

Farmers and Harvesting

Makar Sankranti often coincides with the completion of winter harvest. Families offer gratitude for the bounty of the fields and seek good fortune for future crops.

Worship Cows

In many regions, Go Puja honors cows and bulls for their role in agriculture, with prayers for health and future harvests.
