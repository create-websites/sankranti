History & Significance

1

The Legend of Dulla Bhatti

Lohri is renowned for the tale of Dulla Bhatti, known as the 'Robin Hood of Punjab', who saved girls from being sold into slavery and ensured they were married with respect and dowry.

2

Harvest Connection

It signifies the conclusion of winter's peak and is connected to the Rabi crop season (including wheat and mustard), honoring the dedication of farmers and the anticipation of a bountiful harvest.

3

The Holy Bonfire

During Lohri, the bonfire represents Agni and the sun's coming back. Around it, individuals offer sesame, jaggery, and peanuts as prayers for prosperity.

A Song of Dulla Bhatti

"Sundri-Mundri hoye! Tera kaun vicharaa! Dulla Bhatti wala!"

During Lohri, the hero's memory is kept alive as this beloved folk song resonates throughout all neighborhoods.
