Lohri Sweets
Festival of Warmth

Sweetness & Tradition

Lohri is a celebration of thankfulness and the bounty of the season. Discover traditional sweets, contemporary healthy options, and the rich cultural importance of Sesame (Til).

Celebrating Abundance

Food, particularly treats made from sesame seeds and jaggery, is a key element of the festivities. Originally, Lohri dishes aimed to provide warmth and abundance in the cold season, representing prosperity and unity. Nowadays, many households seek healthier alternatives that still capture the cultural significance.

Easy Homemade Lohri Sweets

Til-Gur Gajak

Sesame Brittle

Ingredients:

  • White sesame seeds – 1 cup
  • Jaggery (gur) – ¾ cup
  • Ghee – 1 tsp

Method:

  1. Dry roast sesame seeds until golden.
  2. Melt jaggery on low heat until syrupy.
  3. Mix seeds into jaggery quickly.
  4. Spread thin on greased surface.
  5. Cool and break into pieces.
Why it’s special: Provides warmth and energy, perfect for winter.

Rewari

Sesame & Jaggery Balls

Ingredients:

  • Sesame seeds – 1 cup
  • Jaggery – ¾ cup
  • Ghee – 1 tsp

Method:

  1. Roast sesame seeds until aromatic.
  2. Melt jaggery with ghee.
  3. Mix seeds and shape into small balls.
  4. Let them cool completely.
Why it’s special: Symbolizes sweetness, prosperity, and sharing.

Til Laddoo

No-Mold Version

Ingredients:

  • Sesame seeds – 1 cup
  • Powdered jaggery – ¾ cup

Method:

  1. Roast sesame seeds & grind coarsely.
  2. Mix with jaggery powder.
  3. Shape into laddoos while warm.
Why it’s special: An easy, quick recipe ideal for busy households.

Modern Healthy Swaps

Although traditional Lohri dishes are healthy, modern lifestyles often prefer lighter options that still maintain the festive atmosphere and promote wellness.

Til & Dry Fruit Energy Balls

Made with dates, almonds, and walnuts.

Rich in protein, calcium, and beneficial fats. Perfect for children and health-conscious individuals.

Roasted Peanuts & Jaggery

A simple crumble.

Maintains classic tastes without using processed sugar. Packed with fiber and plant-based protein.

Popcorn with Sesame Glaze

Air-popped and light.

A contemporary spin providing crunch with fewer calories than rich desserts.

Til Chikki with Honey

Low Sugar Option.

Honey or date syrup can help lower the glycemic load of a dish while maintaining its comforting qualities.

Why Sesame (Til) Is Important

Cultural Significance

  • Symbolizes warmth, longevity, and protection.
  • Believed to absorb negativity.
  • Offered to the bonfire to thank the Sun God.

Seasonal Importance

  • Generates internal heat during harsh winters.
  • Strengthens bones (high calcium).
  • Boosts immunity and digestion.

Ayurvedic Perspective

  • Balances cold (vata) in winter.
  • Nourishes skin and joints.
  • Promotes overall vitality.
