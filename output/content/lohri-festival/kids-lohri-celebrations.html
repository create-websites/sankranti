Lohri for Kids
Ages 4–12 Years

Fun, Learning & Joy

Lohri is a time for children to experience joy, warmth, and unity. Discover fun ways to create lasting memories with our interactive checklist featuring crafts, stories, and games.

Connecting Kids to Culture

This guide highlights enjoyable activities that impart tradition through hands-on experiences. From safe campfires to delicious treats, each activity fosters cultural understanding and imagination.

Be Creative

Participate in creative activities such as crafting paper bonfires or designing greeting cards.

Move & Groove

Learn traditional songs and simple Bhangra steps to build confidence.

Story Time

Engage with your heritage by sharing tales like Dulla Bhatti and your family's past.
