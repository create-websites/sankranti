Corporate Lohri
Workplace Culture

Warmth at Work

Promote a sense of unity and appreciation in the workplace with a fun and inclusive Lohri celebration. Increase team spirit and foster a welcoming atmosphere through this professional and engaging event.

Why Celebrate at the Office?

Lohri celebrations in the office don't have to be grandiose; even small activities can ensure a memorable festival that brings moments of joy amidst the daily grind.

Team Bonding

Fosters inclusivity and enhances relationships among coworkers through shared holiday traditions.

Cultural Diversity

Acknowledges the varied employee backgrounds, fostering a positive and inclusive environment.

Morale Boost

Provides a refreshing escape from the daily grind, infusing the workday with renewed vigor and optimism.

Professional Fun

Created to be both compelling and appropriate, guaranteeing the well-being and dignity of every employee.

Creative & Safe

Office-Friendly Ideas

7 Easy ways to infuse your workplace with holiday cheer.

Symbolic Bonfire

Make a safe 'flame' with LED lights, paper flames, and decorative diyas. Enjoy photo ops without the smoke hazard.

Lohri Snack Counter

Create a table showcasing Rewari, Gajak, Popcorn, and Til-Laddoos and provide labels explaining their cultural importance for educational purposes.

Festive Playlist

In public areas, play gentle Punjabi folk music such as 'Sundar Mundariye Ho' at a suitable volume for office settings.

Traditional Attire

Encourage the wearing of ethnic attire such as Phulkari dupattas or Kurtas, but make it optional for comfort.

Quick Activities

Organize a 10-minute quiz on Lohri customs or a competition for the best desk decoration. Give out small treats instead of lavish rewards.

Photo Booth

Create a corner adorned with props such as a dhol, phulkari fabrics, and signs, perfect for inclusion in internal newsletters.

Goodie Bags

Give out mini bags containing peanuts, popcorn, and a greeting card. Ideal for engaging busy or remote employees.

Inclusive Celebration Tips

Explain the Meaning

Explain the importance of Lohri to non-Punjabi colleagues to foster cultural understanding.

Keep it Voluntary

In order to ensure everyone feels at ease, participation in dress-up or games should be voluntary.

Mindful Snacking

Provide snack options that are vegetarian and allergen-friendly to ensure inclusivity in food choices.

Focus on Culture

Steer clear of rigid religious customs and instead emphasize the cultural festivities of abundance and camaraderie.
