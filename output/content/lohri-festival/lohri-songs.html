Lohri Melodies
Heartbeat of Punjab

Songs of Fire & Folklore

Lohri songs serve as a living record of the past, honoring Dulla Bhatti's tale and commemorating unity, courage, and appreciation with poignant, uncomplicated lyrics.

More Than Just Melodies

Sung by the bonfire or during Lohri collection, these songs bridge generations to their cultural heritage, imparting lessons of bravery, kindness, and belief.

History

Honoring folk heroes like Dulla Bhatti who fought for justice.

Harvest

Expressing gratitude to nature for fertility and abundance.

Blessings

Showering love and good wishes on newborns and newlyweds.

Legendary Verses

The Most Famous Songs

1. Sundar Mundariye Ho

ਸੁੰਦਰ ਮੁੰਦਰਿਏ ਹੋ!
ਤੇਰਾ ਕੌਣ ਵਿਚਾਰਾ ਹੋ?
ਦੁੱਲਾ ਭੱਟੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਹੋ!
ਦੁੱਲੇ ਦੀ ਧੀ ਵਿਆਹੀ ਹੋ!

Translation:

Oh lovely Sundri, who is there to watch over you? Dulla Bhatti is your safeguard, ensuring his daughter's marriage with honor...

Significance: Honors Dulla Bhatti for his protection of orphan girls and invokes blessings for fostering social responsibility.

2. Dulla Bhatti Ho

ਦੁੱਲਾ ਭੱਟੀ ਹੋ!
ਸ਼ੇਰ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਾ ਹੋ!
ਧਨੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਲੁੱਟਿਆ ਹੋ!
ਗਰੀਬਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਹੋ!

Translation:

O Dulla Bhatti, the fearless Lion of Punjab, who stole from the wealthy and shared with the less fortunate!

Significance: Honors the fight against oppression, preserving the bravery and righteousness of Punjabi tales.

3. Ho Tera Kaun Vichara Ho

ਹੋ ਤੇਰਾ ਕੌਣ ਵਿਚਾਰਾ ਹੋ?
ਅੰਬਰਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਉਤਰਿਆ ਹੋ!
ਰੱਬ ਨੇ ਭੇਜਿਆ ਹੋ!

Meaning:

'Who will shield you? From above, God has dispatched a protector.'

Significance: Shows belief in divine protection and hope, especially important during brutal winters when survival relied on nature.

Nature & Play

Gratitude & Joy

Harvest Gratitude

ਆਦਿ ਆਉਣੀ ਲੋਹੜੀ ਵੇ,
ਵੇਖੋ ਜੱਟਾਂ ਦੀ ਜੋੜੀ ਵੇ...

Lohri is here, Witness the solidarity of farmers, Share sesame, indulge in sweets...

Expresses hope for prosperity and thanks nature for the harvest.

Children's Collection

ਲੋਹੜੀ ਆਈ, ਲੋਹੜੀ ਆਈ,
ਜੀਵਣ ਜਗਾਇਆ, ਫਸਲ ਲਿਆਈ...

Lohri is here, Celebrating life and bounty, Bring coins, sesame, and treats, Or face the flames' fury!

Lighthearted verses promoting sharing, generosity, and festive fun.

Songs for New Beginnings

Linking Lohri to fertility, the continuation of family, and the joy of life.

ਪੁੱਤ ਜੰਮਿਆ ਘਰ ਖੁਸ਼ੀ ਆਈ,
ਲੋਹੜੀ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਰੌਸ਼ਨਾਈ
ਲੰਮੀ ਉਮਰ, ਚੜ੍ਹਦੀ ਕਲਾ,
ਵੱਸਦਾ ਰਹੇ ਇਹ ਘਰ ਸਦਾ

A new son brings joy to the household, as the Lohri fire glows warmly. May blessings of longevity and prosperity always be upon this home.
