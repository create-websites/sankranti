Shradh / Pitru Paksha Dates (2026)

In 2026, Pitru Paksha begins on September 26 and concludes on October 10. Shradh is traditionally performed on the tithi (lunar day) that matches the ancestor’s passing.
Best time
Aparahna Kaal (afternoon), typically 1:30 PM – 3:55 PM
Diet
Sattvic diet (avoid onion, garlic, non-veg)
Core components
Tarpan & food offering (Brahman Bhoj)

Notes

Suggested checklist
  • Perform rituals during Aparahna Kaal (afternoon).
  • Offer water (Tarpan) with remembrance.
  • Offer food / charity (Brahman Bhoj) as customary.
  • Keep meals sattvic for the day.
Important observances
  • Maha Bharani Shradh (Sept 29): highly auspicious; believed as meritorious as performing Shradh in Gaya.
  • Avidhava Navami (Oct 4): dedicated to deceased mothers and married women who passed away before their husbands.
  • Sarva Pitru Amavasya (Mahalaya) (Oct 10): most significant day; intended for all ancestors if tithi is unknown.
