Welcome to the 2026 interactive Panchang. The Hindu calendar is lunisolar, meaning dates are calculated based on the positions of both the Sun and the Moon. Because Tithis (lunar days) depend on the exact moment of sunrise in your specific location, festival dates can sometimes vary by a day between India, the USA, and Europe.
