Why Chaturthi and Panchami Shraddha Share September 30 in 2026

The short answer

In the New Delhi reference calendar, both observances are assigned toWednesday, September 30, 2026. The reason is not a printing error: both tithis intersect the afternoon decision period used for Shraddha date selection.

Why simple day-counting fails

If someone labels Pratipada as “day 1” and then just counts civil days, the sequence becomes misleading after September 30. Panchami does not simply move to October 1; Shashthi is the named Shraddha for October 1 in the New Delhi reference calendar.

What to do in practice

Find the ancestor’s death tithi first, then verify that tithi in the local Panchang for the city where the observance is taking place. Do not choose a date by ordinal day number alone.

How Is the Correct Shraddha Tithi Chosen During Pitru Paksha?

A practical selection sequence

  1. Identify the ancestor’s lunar death tithi if known.
  2. Locate that tithi within the 2026 Pitru Paksha calendar.
  3. Set the Panchang to the city where the observance will occur.
  4. Check the local afternoon/tithi assignment and any special family rule.
  5. Confirm with family or priestly guidance when traditions differ.

Why the Gregorian anniversary is not enough

The death anniversary’s civil date does not reliably tell you the Shraddha tithi. A tithi is based on the lunar phase and shifts through the civil calendar each year.
