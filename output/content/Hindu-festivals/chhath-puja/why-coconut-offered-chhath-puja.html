Why Is Coconut Offered During Chhath Puja?

Examine the provided description of the coconut as Shriphal in Chhath Puja, highlighting its devotional sanctity, protective outer shell, and the prayers offered for the family's welfare.

The Shriphal: The Ultimate Offering

The coconut is known as Shriphal (the fruit of the gods) and serves as a vital offering in nearly all significant Hindu ceremonies, including Chhath Puja.

The Symbolism of the Coconut

  • Absolute Purity: The liquid within a green coconut is entirely isolated from the external environment. It represents the purest form of fluid, untouched by human contact, making it a perfect gift to the Sun God.
  • Fertility and Life: The tough outer shell safeguarding the soft, life-sustaining water within symbolizes the womb. During Chhath, women particularly present coconuts in their prayers for the health, safety, and fertility of their children.
