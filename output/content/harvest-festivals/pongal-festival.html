Pongal Festival

A Joyous Harvest Celebration of Southern India

A Time for Gratitude and Renewal

Pongal, a lively four-day harvest festival, is joyfully observed in Tamil Nadu, India. It serves as a thanksgiving to the Sun God, Surya, for a prosperous harvest. The celebration signifies the close of the winter solstice and the sun's shift northward, symbolizing growth and renewal. Loved ones come together to celebrate, share meals, and express gratitude to nature.
